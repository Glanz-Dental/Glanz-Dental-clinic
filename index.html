Our Services

Dental care for different needs.

Explore the treatments currently highlighted at GLANZ Dental Clinic.

DENTAL VENEERS IMAGE
01

Dental Veneers

Your smile — refined, not replaced.

If the shape, shade, or balance of your teeth is affecting your smile, veneers can help improve its overall appearance with the goal of creating a natural, balanced result that suits you.

DENTAL IMPLANTS IMAGE
02

Dental Implants

A missing tooth should not hold your smile back.

Dental implants are an option for replacing missing teeth and can help restore both the appearance of your smile and everyday dental function.

ROOT CANAL IMAGE
03

Root Canal Treatment

Tooth pain should not become your new normal.

When appropriate for the case, root canal treatment may be completed in a single visit. The aim is to treat the affected tooth, relieve the source of pain, and preserve the natural tooth whenever possible.

ORTHODONTICS IMAGE
04

Orthodontics

A more balanced smile starts with the right plan.

Orthodontic treatment can improve tooth alignment and bite. The treatment plan is selected according to the individual needs of each case.

ORAL SURGERY IMAGE
05

Oral & Maxillofacial Surgery

When your case needs surgery, specialist care matters.

Surgical conditions involving the mouth and jaws require careful assessment and specialized management to determine the appropriate approach for each patient.
