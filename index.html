Dental Implants and Oral and Maxillofacial Surgery

If you have a missing tooth or need oral surgery your care begins with an examination and a review of your X-rays to determine the right approach for your case.

The treatment plan is explained before you begin. Careful attention is given to appropriate anesthesia and your comfort during treatment. You also receive clear aftercare instructions.

Veneers and Cosmetic Dentistry

Cosmetic dentistry does not mean giving everyone the same smile. The shape and shade are selected to suit your teeth and facial features so the result looks natural.

Before treatment begins you will understand what can be improved and which option suits your case. This may include veneers cosmetic restorations or smile design.

Root Canal Treatment

When decay or infection reaches the inside of a tooth root canal treatment helps remove the source of pain and preserve the natural tooth whenever possible.

The tooth is examined first. Then the treatment steps and expected number of visits are explained. Some cases can be completed in one visit while others may need more depending on the condition of the tooth.

Appropriate anesthesia is used with attention to your comfort throughout treatment.

Pediatric Dentistry

Children need a calm approach that suits their age and helps them feel safe and more comfortable with dental treatment.

The child’s needs are assessed carefully. The treatment plan is explained clearly to the family with attention to restoring both the function and appearance of the teeth.

Orthodontics

Orthodontic treatment helps improve the position of the teeth and the way the upper and lower teeth meet. This can improve the smile and make daily cleaning easier.

After the examination the dentist explains the most suitable treatment plan and the expected duration and follow-up before treatment begins.

Frequently Asked Questions

How can I keep my teeth healthy?+

Brush twice a day with fluoride toothpaste. Clean between your teeth and limit sugary food and drinks. Regular dental check-ups can help find problems before they become more serious.

What is tooth decay and how is it treated?+

Tooth decay happens when part of the tooth becomes damaged. A simple case may need a filling. If the decay reaches the inside of the tooth root canal treatment may be needed. The right treatment is determined after an examination.

What are the signs of gum inflammation?+

Common signs include bleeding while brushing and red or swollen gums and persistent bad breath or gum discomfort. If these symptoms continue it is best to book an examination before the problem becomes more serious.

What can cause problems around a dental implant?+

Poor cleaning and gum inflammation and smoking and uncontrolled blood sugar or too much pressure on the implant can cause problems. Good daily care and regular follow-up can help protect the implant.
