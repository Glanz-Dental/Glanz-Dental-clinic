FAQs

Quick answers before your visit

How can I keep my teeth healthy?+

Brush twice a day with fluoride toothpaste. Clean between your teeth and limit sugary food and drinks. Regular dental check-ups can help find problems before they become more serious.

What is tooth decay and how is it treated?+

A simple case may need a filling. If decay reaches the inside of the tooth root canal treatment may be needed. The right treatment is determined after an examination.

What are the signs of gum inflammation?+

Common signs include bleeding while brushing and red or swollen gums and persistent bad breath or gum discomfort. If symptoms continue it is best to book an examination.
